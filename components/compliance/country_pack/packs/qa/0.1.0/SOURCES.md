# Qatar country pack 0.1.0: sources and evidence

Status: tested (structure only, schema and unit tests pass). Every fact in `pack.json` is UNVERIFIED (`evidenceStatus: unverified`). Source: the CMP-030 research, [docs/compliance/qatar-research.md](../../../../../../docs/compliance/qatar-research.md). Access date for every URL: 2026-11-18. ## Facts the pack schema does not hold (UNVERIFIED, for later packs and components)
- Data protection: Law No. 13 of 2016, regulator NCSA, 72-hour breach notice (unconfirmed against the text), no general localisation duty found. Source: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=QA&t=law (2026-11-18).
- Corporate income tax (10% on the foreign-owned share) and withholding tax (5% to non-residents): no rates are stored here. Source: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/qatar/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/qatar/corporate/withholding-taxes (2026-11-18).
- Accounting standard: IFRS. Source: https://www.ifac.org/about-ifac/membership/profile/qatar (2026-11-18).
