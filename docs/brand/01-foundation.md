# 01 Foundation (PDF p.2)

Orrery is a working model of your business that you can run forward. The brand must make an owner feel three things at once: **this is calm, this is exact, and I am in control.**

- **Idea.** A precise, living model of the business: built from vetted parts, tested on a full month, then run and improved through conversation.
- **Promise.** An ERP that fits how you already work, proven before go-live, in Arabic or English.
- **Audience.** Owners of businesses with 10 to 100 staff in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Time-poor, distrust slow, complicated or generic software, care about ZATCA compliance, accuracy and control.
- **Personality.** Direct, confident, exact. Never hyped, cute or technical for show.

| | Corporate ERP (SAP, Oracle, Odoo) | Cheap accounting apps | Orrery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Months, consultants | Minutes, but generic | Days, built from your own description |
| Fit | You adapt to the system | You work around gaps | The system fits the business |
| Confidence before go-live | Training and hope | None | A simulated month |
| Feel | Heavy, corporate | Cheap, cluttered | Calm, precise, trustworthy |

The brand never uses purple-blue gradients, sparkles, glowing orbs, robot or brain icons, neural-network imagery, cartoon planets, stock-photo styling or template layouts.

> Claims "Days" and "A simulated month" must not be published until the product can prove them (see `10-open-items.md`).
