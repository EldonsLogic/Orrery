# 02 Logo: Nuqta S (ADR-0066)

Source: Seshna logo kit "Nuqta S", draft v3, adopted by the founders on 2026-11-23. Files in `logo/` are generated by `tools/build_logo.py`; never redraw or rebuild a lockup by hand. Trademark search still pending (`10-open-items.md`).

## Name
- Always **Seshna**, in Latin letters with a capital S, in every language, layout and channel. Domain: seshna.com.
- **Never written in Arabic script**: not in the logo, not in Arabic copy, not in search or store text. There is no Arabic wordmark and no Arabic lockup; the Latin lockup is used unchanged in Arabic layouts.
- **In Arabic text** keep the Latin name and isolate it as left-to-right (`<bdi>Seshna</bdi>` in HTML, or the i18n library's isolation).
- **In running text** plain "Seshna": never all caps, never all lowercase, no TM or (R) until registered, never translated.

## Mark construction
The scribe's rhombic dot (a square turned 45 degrees), cut through its centre along the reed pen's angle. Each half ends in a round stroke at the opposite end, so the pair reads as an S.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Grid | 256 units |
| Nuqta diamond | Half-diagonal 112 u; vertices at the top and bottom of the mark |
| Cut | Through the centre, lower left to upper right (the pen angle); width 18 u |
| Halves | Each half is a bar; its outer end is a half circle with radius = half the bar height (35.1 u). Upper half: left end. Lower half: right end |
| Masters | Flat paths only: no live text, transforms or clip paths |

- Never mirror, flip or rotate the mark, including in right-to-left layouts: a mirrored mark reads as a reversed S.
- **Small-size cut.** Below 24 px the cut widens to 30 u so the gap survives at 16 px (`mark-small.svg`, `mark-small-dark.svg`). The favicon and the app icons use it.

## Wordmark
Cairo Bold (700), tracking -0.5% em, kerning from the font (HarfBuzz), outlined to paths (`wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg`). Never retype it in a font. Cairo's angled stroke ends repeat the cut of the mark. Cairo is also the heading typeface (`04-typography.md`).

| Lockup | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal, primary | `lockup-primary.svg` | Default: app header, website, documents, email |
| Horizontal, dark | `lockup-dark.svg` (on a #0E1117 panel with clear space) | Dark mode and dark surfaces |
| Horizontal, on Cobalt | `lockup-on-cobalt.svg` (all white) | Marketing moments only |
| Stacked, primary and dark | `lockup-stacked-primary.svg`, `lockup-stacked-dark.svg` | Square spaces: social, splash screen, covers |
| Mark alone | `mark-*.svg`, `app-icon*.svg` | App icon (white on Cobalt), favicon, avatar |

Proportions (X = cap height of the wordmark): horizontal mark height 1.6 X, centred on the middle of the cap height, gap from the mark's edge 0.6 X. Stacked mark height 2.2 X, gap 0.6 X. In RTL layouts the lockup is not mirrored: the mark stays left of the name; place the whole lockup at the start of the layout (right in Arabic). No new lockups.

## Clear space and minimum sizes
Clear space: 1 X on every side of a lockup or wordmark. Mark alone: one third of its height.

| Asset | Digital | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Wordmark alone | 64 px wide | 16 mm wide |
| Mark, master | 24 px | 7 mm |
| Mark, small-size cut | 16 to 23 px | 5 to 7 mm |

- **Coral never appears in the logo** and never on the wordmark. Single-colour versions keep both halves and both round ends.
- Contrast (from the kit, re-checked by the colour tokens): Cobalt mark on #F3F4F6 6.84:1, on white 7.53:1; Carbon wordmark on #F3F4F6 16.61:1; #86A0FF on #0E1117 7.64:1; Chalk on #0E1117 15.70:1; white on Cobalt 7.53:1.

## Misuse
- Do not stretch, condense, rotate, tilt or mirror the mark (including in RTL).
- Do not recolour outside the table; no Coral or other accent on the wordmark; no gradients, glows, shadows, outlines or 3D.
- Do not retype the wordmark, use another typeface, or set it in all caps or all lowercase.
- No Arabic-script version of the name and no translated name.
- Do not change the gap between mark and wordmark, resize parts, or make new lockups.
- Do not split the halves apart, colour them differently, or square off the round ends.
- Do not put the mark in a container other than the app icon tile, or on busy photos or colours that fail the contrast table.

## Status and Progress marks (product)
- **Status:** open (outline) = not started; one solid half and one outline half = drafted by the AI; solid = approved by a person. Always with a text label (rule 40).
- **Progress:** five marks for Discover, Blueprint, Build, Simulate, Live.
- **Bullets:** a small solid Cobalt mark; Coral marks at most one item per view, always with a text label.
These are product graphics, drawn from the same geometry; exact sizes and states are not yet specified (`10-open-items.md`).
