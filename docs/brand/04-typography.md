# 04 Typography (PDF p.9)

Two families cover both scripts, both free for commercial use under the SIL Open Font License (as stated in the PDF; UNVERIFIED) and on Google Fonts.
- **Alexandria.** Wordmark and headings. Geometric, with a Kufi-derived Arabic and flat terminals that echo the quadrant's straight edges. Headings at 500 to 600.
- **Readex Pro.** Interface and body text. Built for legibility in Latin and Arabic. Body at 400, labels and buttons at 500.

| Style | Family | Size / line height, Latin | Size / line height, Arabic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Alexandria 600 | 40 / 48 px | 40 / 60 px |
| Heading 1 | Alexandria 600 | 32 / 40 px | 32 / 48 px |
| Heading 2 | Alexandria 500 | 24 / 32 px | 24 / 36 px |
| Heading 3 | Alexandria 500 | 20 / 28 px | 20 / 32 px |
| Body | Readex Pro 400 | 16 / 24 px | 16 / 28 px |
| Small | Readex Pro 400 | 14 / 20 px | 14 / 24 px |
| Caption | Readex Pro 400 | 12 / 16 px | 12 / 20 px |

- **Arabic** uses the same sizes with more line height (taller ascenders and descenders). Never fake an italic or slant; emphasis comes from weight.
- **Numerals.** Tabular figures for amounts, tables and invoices. Default to Western digits (0-9) in both languages for amounts, dates and document numbers; to be confirmed with pilot customers.
- **Currency.** "SAR 12,480.00" in English and "12,480.00 ر.س" in Arabic. The PDF says Saudi Arabia and the UAE both introduced new currency symbols in 2025; use them only where the fonts support them, otherwise the codes. (Claim UNVERIFIED.)
- **No all-caps labels.** Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, labels.

Specimen in the PDF: "Describe it. We build it. You run it." / "صِفه لنا، نبنيه، وتديره أنت."; body "Invoice INV-0418 was reported on 6 November. SAR 12,480.00".
