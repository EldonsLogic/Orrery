# 07 Graphic elements and iconography (PDF p.12)

The Nuqta S mark (`02-logo.md`) is the source of every brand graphic. Everything else is real content, product screens and photography.
- **Status marks.** Open = not started; one solid half and one outline half = drafted by the AI; solid = approved by a person. Always with a text label.
- **Progress marks.** Five marks for Discover, Blueprint, Build, Simulate, Live.
- **Bullets.** A small solid Cobalt mark; Coral marks at most one item per view, always with a text label.
- **Retired.** The v1 quadrant device and quadrant progress graphic went with the Quadrant mark (ADR-0066). No replacement device is defined yet (`10-open-items.md`).
- **Grid and corners.** 8 px base grid. Corner radius 8 px on controls, 12 px on cards and panels. Brand graphics keep sharp corners.
- **Icons.** One outline set at 24 px with a 1.75 px stroke and rounded joins, for example Lucide (ISC licence, with Feather-derived icons under MIT; checked 2026-11-18, see the licence register). Directional icons mirror in RTL layouts.
- **Photography.** Real owner-led businesses in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Egypt and Qatar at work: shops, warehouses, workshops, offices, in natural light. No posed stock scenes, futuristic screens or robots.
- **Never.** Gradients, glows, sparkles, orbs, 3D planets, neural networks, Islamic geometric patterns used as decoration.

States are built in the product (`09-applications.md`); sizes and stroke are inferred, see `10-open-items.md`.
