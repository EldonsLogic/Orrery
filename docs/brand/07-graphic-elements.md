# 07 Graphic elements and iconography (PDF p.12)

The quadrant is the only brand graphic: quarter discs, 90 degree arcs and the radial slot, always anchored to a corner. Everything else is real content, product screens and photography.
- **Quadrant device.** A large cropped quarter disc anchored to a corner of a slide, post or page, in Cobalt or Carbon at low opacity. Unlike the logo mark, the device may turn in 90 degree steps to fit any corner.
- **Progress.** A quadrant filling sector by sector can show progress through the simulated month.
- **Grid and corners.** 8 px base grid. Corner radius 8 px on controls, 12 px on cards and panels. Brand graphics keep sharp corners.
- **Icons.** One outline set at 24 px with a 1.75 px stroke and rounded joins, for example Lucide (ISC licence, UNVERIFIED). Directional icons mirror in RTL layouts.
- **Photography.** Real owner-led businesses in Saudi Arabia and the UAE at work: shops, warehouses, workshops, offices, in natural light. No posed stock scenes, futuristic screens or robots.
- **Never.** Gradients, glows, sparkles, orbs, 3D planets, neural networks, Islamic geometric patterns used as decoration.

"Low opacity" has no stated value (see `10-open-items.md`).
