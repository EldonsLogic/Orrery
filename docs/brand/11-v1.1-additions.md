# 11 v1.1 additions (PROPOSED)

Status: proposed. Owner: unassigned (founders accept or reject each section).
Nothing here is in "Brand guidelines v1". Files 01 to 09 and `tokens/tokens.json` are unchanged. Every value comes from `tokens/tokens.proposed.json` (generated by `tools/build_proposed_tokens.py`) and is verified by `tools/check_proposed.py` (200 contrast pairs, both themes, 0 failures). Arabic strings marked ⚑ need native-speaker review. Fonts, Lucide icon names and store icon specs were not checked online: UNVERIFIED (no network, 2026-11-06).

Derivation rule for everything below: calm, exact, restrained. Cobalt only where something is actionable, Coral for one highlight, sentence case, 8 px grid, radius 8 controls / 12 cards, brand graphics sharp, no gradients, glows, decorative shadows or 3D, status colour always with icon and label, Arabic and English equal, RTL mirrors layout but never the logo, Western digits tabular.

## 1. Spacing, breakpoints, elevation
| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| `--space-1` to `--space-12` | 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96 px for 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 (multiples of 8) |
| Half steps | `--space-0_5` 4 px (icon to label), `--space-1_5` 12 px (cell padding). Nothing else off-grid |
| Breakpoints (min width) | compact 0, medium 600, expanded 840, large 1200, xlarge 1600 (literal numbers in media queries) |

| Breakpoint | Layout |
|---|---|
| compact | One column. Navigation is a drawer plus an optional 64 px bottom bar. Tables become cards or scroll with a sticky first column |
| medium | 72 px collapsed rail. Drawers 400 px |
| expanded | 256 px sidebar. Two columns allowed |
| large, xlarge | Same, content max width 1440 px; reading and chat column max 720 px |

**Elevation without decoration.** Separation comes from surface and border, in this order.
| Level | Use | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Page | `--color-background`, no border |
| 1 | Cards, panels, tables | `--color-surface`, 1 px `--color-border`, no shadow |
| 2 | Menu, popover, modal, drawer, toast | `--color-surface-raised`, 1 px `--color-border`, plus `--shadow-overlay` in light only |

The one allowed shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(14,17,23,0.16)`, only on level 2, because a floating layer over scrolling content of the same tone needs to read as in front. Never on cards, buttons, inputs or images. Dark mode has none: raised surface `#21252D` (Panel mixed 5% toward Chalk) and the border do the work. Scrim behind modals and drawers: Carbon deep at 48% (light) or 64% (dark).

**Status pills, a v1 gap.** v1 says "icon and label in the status colour on a 14% tint". On its own tint the full colour reaches only 4.41:1 for Jade on Panel in light, 3.68:1 on a selected row and 3.51:1 on a pressed row; dark Crimson reaches 3.95:1 on a selected row. Proposed: the icon keeps the full status colour (3:1, passes); the label uses `--color-success-pill`, `--color-warning-pill`, `--color-danger-pill` (status colour mixed toward text in 5% steps until 4.5:1 on every row state): light #13654E, #824905, #A81E35; dark #4CC79A, #F2A541, #F891A2. Pill label worst case now 4.76 to 4.96:1.

## 6. App icon geometry
Tile 64 units, corner radius 14 (21.88%, rounded rectangle, not a squircle), mark width 56%, nudge 1.5 up and 1.5 right (2.34% of the tile). Reasoning: the area centroid of the two sectors is at (14.42, 17.64) against the bounding-box centre (16.25, 15.75); full mass centring would move the mark (2.92, 3.01) units of 64. The 1.5 nudge is 51% of that: halfway between geometric and visual centring. Safe zone: the farthest point of the mark is 25.43 of 64 from the centre; the 40% radius circle is 25.6. Fits a 80% maskable safe circle (UNVERIFIED rule). Stores that apply their own mask get a full-bleed square (`logo/png/apple-touch-icon.png`). Light and dark PNGs: `logo/png/` via `tools/build_icons.py`.

## 7. Form controls
Height 40 px (compact 32, touch 48), radius 8, 1 px `--color-border-control` (4.47:1 on Panel light, 4.49:1 dark; 3:1 needed). Label above, small 500; helper caption in muted text; focus ring as section 3. Required fields say "(required)" in words, never an asterisk alone.
| Control | Spec |
|---|---|
| Input, select, textarea | Textarea min height 96. Select shows a chevron that does not mirror. Error: 2 px Crimson border, a warning icon and a message that names the problem and the fix; the field value stays |
| Checkbox, radio | 20 px box inside a 40 px hit area; checked = Cobalt fill with label-colour check; a dash for mixed |
| Switch | 40 x 24 track, 16 thumb. Thumb goes to the end edge when on (mirrors in RTL). Label beside it states the setting, not "on/off" |
| Date field | Typed or picked; accepts Western and Arabic-Indic digits and stores Western. Gregorian default, Hijri as an optional second line (open question). Week start per tenant (default Sunday, UNVERIFIED) |
| Amount field | Tabular figures, number end-aligned (right in LTR, left in RTL), number always LTR-isolated (`dir="ltr"`) even inside Arabic, 2 decimals for SAR and AED, `,` thousands. Currency code before the number in English ("SAR 12,480.00"), after it in Arabic ("12,480.00 ر.س"). Pasted Arabic-Indic digits normalise to Western. Negative: leading minus in the LTR isolate |

**Approval card** (AGENTS.md rule: agents draft, humans approve). A draft action never runs until a person with the right role approves it.
1. Header: caption "Draft action", title naming the action, pill "Waiting for approval" (Ochre).
2. Summary: one sentence with exact numbers.
3. What changes: a table of object, before, after, with a plus or minus icon and the words "New" or "Removed", plus a totals row.
4. Impact: what it touches ("Ledger, November VAT return") and whether it can be undone.
5. Source line with a link to the evidence.
6. Actions: **Approve and post** (the one primary), **Reject** (secondary, optional reason), "Change draft" (link).
| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Pending | Both buttons live |
| Approved | Read-only receipt, Jade pill "Approved by you, 14:02", stays in the thread for audit |
| Rejected by user | Neutral pill (not Crimson: nothing failed), reason kept |
| Failed | Crimson pill with the reason and the fix, "Retry" |
| Stale | Buttons disabled, text says what changed, "Refresh draft" |
| No permission | Buttons replaced by "Needs approval from an accountant" |

Rules: never auto-approve, no countdowns, a confirmation modal only for irreversible or over-threshold actions, Enter never approves by itself.

Done sectors Cobalt; the current sector fills outward from the corner; waiting sectors outlined in `--color-border-control`. ## 12. Charts
Cobalt for the main series, Coral for the one point that matters (v1). Up to 6 categorical series; status colours only when the data is a status. Series 1 to 3 are brand colours; 4 to 6 were chosen by script to keep the status hues (Crimson, Jade, Ochre) free and to maximise separation under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia.
| # | Light | Dark | Min contrast on background, Panel, raised (3:1) light / dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | #2443D0 | #86A0FF | 6.84 / 6.21 |
| 2 | #E0552C | #FF8A63 | 3.47 / 6.63 |
| 3 | #12151B | #E8EAEF | 16.61 / 12.76 |
| 4 | #0095A1 | #2B8F9A | 3.28 / 4.02 |
| 5 | #5C4300 | #A1790C | 8.45 / 3.84 |
| 6 | #97386B | #F3A6CA | 6.17 / 8.13 |
| Comparison | #8B8C8F (text at 49%) | #6B6E75 (Chalk at 40%) | 3.06 / 3.01 |

v1's "Carbon at 40%" comparison gives 2.58:1 in light, below 3:1; 49% is the lowest alpha that passes. Colour-blind check (OKLab distance, Machado matrices, UNVERIFIED): minimum pairwise 0.118 light and 0.104 dark under the worst simulation; threshold 0.08 is our own.
Rules: colour is never the only cue. Every series also has a marker (circle, square, triangle, diamond, cross, ring) on lines and a pattern (solid, diagonal hatch, dots, cross-hatch, reverse hatch, dashed outline) on fills; label series directly at the line or bar end; legends only when direct labels do not fit; 1 px surface gap between stacked fills; tooltips show exact values; "View as table" is always available. Axes stay left-to-right in RTL; legends and layout mirror. No gradients, shadows or 3D.
