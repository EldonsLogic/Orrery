# KSA chart of accounts and VAT codes

Status: Draft for Tier 3 and Arabic-speaking accountant review

Plan ID: CMP-006

## Chart design

The [Saudi trading chart](../../apps/engine/compliance/ksa/sa_trading_chart_of_accounts.json) follows the Saudi-adopted IFRS for SMEs presentation for an eligible trading or distribution company. It groups current and non-current balances and separates inventory, trade receivables and payables, equity, sales, cost of goods sold and operating expenses. The numbers are platform defaults. SOCPA's endorsement includes Saudi modifications and specifies the transition from the 2015 edition to the 2025 edition for annual periods beginning on or after 2027-01-01, with early application permitted. [SOCPA endorsement, sections 63–64, accessed 2026-11-18](https://www.socpa.org.sa/Socpa/Professional-standards/Accounting-standards/Auditing-Standards-Endorsed.aspx) · [IFRS for SMEs, sections 4, 5, 13 and 17, accessed 2026-11-18](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/supporting-implementation/smes/2025/ifrs-for-smes-standard-markup.pdf)

| Use | Account numbers and native types |
|---|---|
| Cash, bank and trade balances | `1110 Cash`, `1120 Bank`, `1130 Receivable`, `2110 Payable` |
| Perpetual inventory | `1140 Stock`, `2120 Stock Received But Not Billed`, `5110 Cost of Goods Sold`, `5120 Stock Adjustment`, `5130 Expenses Included In Valuation` |
| Assets and valuation | `1210 Fixed Asset`, `1220 Accumulated Depreciation`, `1230 Capital Work in Progress`, `2130 Asset Received But Not Billed`, `5230 Depreciation`, `5260 Expenses Included In Asset Valuation` |
| Equity and sales | `3100`–`3300 Equity`, `4100 Income Account`, `4200 Direct Income` (sales returns and allowances) |
| Opening and rounding | `1190 Temporary`, `2170 Round Off for Opening` (balance sheet), `5310 Round Off` (profit and loss) |
| VAT | `1151`–`1153 Tax` under assets, `2210` and `2220 Tax` under liabilities, `2230 Current Liability` for settlement |

The Arabic-first bilingual node keys become native account names. On a fresh synthetic company, the pinned importer accepts the `tree` object via `create_charts(company, custom_chart=tree)`. Groups follow from child nodes and each root supplies `root_type`. All three JSON files in `apps/engine/compliance/ksa/` carry top-level `"status": "draft"`, `"version": "0.1.0"`, and `"review": "unreviewed"` per [ADR-0033](../decisions/ADR-0033-v1-valuation-rounding-and-arabic-review.md); `sa_trading_chart_of_accounts.json` keeps `"review": "unreviewed"` at top level so `tree` keys remain plain native account names for `create_charts`. The [company defaults](../../apps/engine/compliance/ksa/company_account_defaults.json) wrap Company field mappings under `defaults`, reference account numbers, and cover bilingual names that the engine's name-based fallback cannot resolve. Resolve each number to a unique non-group posting account for the company and set the named Company field to that account's document ID. **Fail-closed loader rule:** if any default account number (or treatment `account_role`) does not resolve to a unique posting account, the loader MUST stop and raise an error rather than falling back to the engine's English name lookup or omitting the setting. Opening entries require balance-sheet accounts. `1170` (Supplier Advances) and `2150` (Customer Advances) exist in the chart as posting accounts and remain unlinked from Company defaults, alongside optional Company advance/deferred-account fields, until accountant review (Q-8). Zakat payable/expense (`CMP-013`), end-of-service benefits, GOSI and salaries payable/expense (`CMP-015`, `CMP-016`), and withholding tax are intentionally deferred from this v1 trading chart under Q-8. ## VAT treatments and sources

The [VAT configuration](../../apps/engine/compliance/ksa/vat_treatments.json) records seven treatments (each with `"review": "unreviewed"`) and all 16 reason codes from the published ZATCA XML code list. For `Z` and `E`, the `en` and `ar` strings store ZATCA's official §11.2.4 `BT-120` wording verbatim (with `"review": "unreviewed"`; note that the published ZATCA v1.2 PDF contains a known typo in `VATEX-SA-34-5` Arabic `القيامة المضافة`, corrected here to `القيمة المضافة`) so `CMP-004` can reuse them directly after accountant sign-off. `VATEX-SA-OOS` stores `null` because it requires a case-specific reason from the taxpayer. ZATCA uses `0` in the invoiced-item percentage for `E`, while the underlying treatment is exempt. For `O`, `null` records that the invoice line has no applicable rate. Because `sale_zero_rated` (`Z`) and `sale_exempt` (`E`) both post to `2210 Output VAT` at `0%` in this trading chart, `CMP-010` (KSA VAT return report) MUST aggregate and split categories `S`, `Z`, `E`, and `O` by tax category / reason code rather than account balance alone (or use split accounts if requested under Q-8). [ZATCA XML implementation standard v1.2, §§8.2 and 11.2.4, BR-Z-05, BR-E-05, BR-KSA-11 and BR-KSA-CL-04, accessed 2026-11-18](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/ar/E-Invoicing/SystemsDevelopers/Documents/20230519_ZATCA_Electronic_Invoice_XML_Implementation_Standard_%20vF.pdf)

|---|---|---|

| Zero-rated sale | `Z`, 0%, 12 eligible reason codes | `2210` at zero (split by category in `CMP-010`). Each `engine_template` with a value describes native sales or purchase tax rows for a single treatment. Provide the company and a title from the label, resolve each `account_role` through `posting_accounts` to the same company's `account_head`, and set the row description from the locale label. The four templates require explicit selection. Imports and reverse charge record account roles for reviewed accounting entries. The engine remains the calculation authority. This directory is self-contained so CMP-029 can wrap it using the CMP-021 country-pack framework.

## Accountant review and verification

- **UNVERIFIED** for adoption. Confirm the Arabic labels, applicable SOCPA edition, deferred statutory/payroll accounts (zakat, end-of-service, GOSI, salaries payable/expense, withholding), unlinked `1170`/`2150` advances and optional defaults, and financial statement mapping, including VAT settlement, refund receivable presentation, opening clearing and equity by entity type (Q-8).
- **UNVERIFIED** end to end. Confirm eligibility and evidence for each reason in the company's actual trade. Verify the invoice component's reason text, mixed-category invoices and credit notes against ZATCA rules. The catalogue alone does not establish transaction eligibility.
- **UNVERIFIED** posting and return treatment. Confirm the workflow for customs declarations, partial and non-deductible input VAT, self-assessed output and settlement against the VAT return (`CMP-010`, including category split on `2210`) (Q-216). Import VAT accounted for through the return with ZATCA approval needs its own workflow. [ZATCA imports and exports guideline, §5.3, accessed 2026-11-18](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Imports-and-Exports-under-VAT-Provision.pdf)

The checks cover draft/version/review metadata, native tree structure, unique numbers, bilingual names, valid posting types, company defaults, verbatim ZATCA BT-120 strings, and VAT links. The four defaults that required explicit links were `round_off_for_opening`, `write_off_account`, `exchange_gain_loss_account` and `disposal_account`.
