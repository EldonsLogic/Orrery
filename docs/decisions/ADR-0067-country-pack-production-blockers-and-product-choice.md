# ADR-0067: Country pack production blockers, product-choice defaults and record checks

Status: Proposed
Owner: partner
Approved by: pending (Eldon answered Q1 and Q2 for both founders on PR #282, 2026-11-23; this ADR records them and awaits the partner's acceptance and Eldon's Tier 3 review)
Plan ID: CMP-036

## Context
[ADR-0063](ADR-0063-country-pack-certification-record.md) left two decisions open: whether `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` may back statutory locked leaves, and how `goLiveBlockers` are handled. Eldon's review of PR #282 (Claude Fable, at 0d38f5a) also found record checks that read more into a file than it says. Eldon decided both open questions on #282 for both founders. This ADR records them and the Low fixes, and amends ADR-0063 (and, through it, [ADR-0059](ADR-0059-draft-country-packs-in-sandbox.md) Decision 2's production rules). ADR-0063 is Accepted and is not edited.

## Decision
All rules apply in production only. Sandbox and test behave as ADR-0059 says. Everything fails closed.

1. **Q1, option C: a named product-choice list per market.** A `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` fact may back a locked pack path only if that path exactly equals a path on the market's product-choice list. A listed path authorises neither an ancestor (listing `tax.roundingRule` does not let a default back `tax`) nor a descendant. The same check applies to a `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` entry in `factsOutsideSchema` that names a locked path. A `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` fact on any other locked path (or on an ancestor of one) is refused, even when another fact also covers the path. Facts outside locked areas are unaffected.
   - The list is data: `components/compliance/country_pack/product_choice_paths.json`, one list per market (`SA`, `AE`, `EG`, `QA`), each entry a `path`, a `reason`, an optional `provisional` note and, on a provisional entry only, a `resolution`. Entries need at least two path segments, so a top-level area is never listable. A missing, malformed, duplicate-key or wrongly shaped file refuses production loading. A market with no entry allows nothing.
   - Shipped entries are the paths the packs may back with a default, and `components/compliance/country_pack/tests/test_production_rules.py` pins them: Saudi `eInvoicing.componentId`, `eInvoicing.mode`; UAE and Qatar `tax.roundingRule` (provisional); Egypt none (its identity-path defaults are refused until its evidence is reworked). This is not every path that defaults today: the SA and AE packs also default `formats.*` and the SA pack `printFormats.*`. Those paths are locked and unlisted, so production refuses them; a founder decision on whether to list them or back them with evidence is pending.
   - **Saudi `tax.roundingRule` is not on the list.** ZATCA's *Electronic Invoice XML Implementation Standard* v1.2, section 10 ("Rounding", page 29), mandates half-up rounding (official, fetched 2026-11-23, [../research/cmp-037-official-sources.md](../research/cmp-037-official-sources.md)). The Saudi value is therefore statutory: it needs `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL` evidence citing section 10, or signed `SECONDARY` evidence, and is never `PLATFORM-DEFAULT`, whatever a `resolution` says. UNVERIFIED: whether the pack's `half_away_from_zero` and half-up differ for negative amounts (credit notes); that is for the accountant. No rounding maths is written or changed here. ([Q-601](../open-questions.md) status is a founder decision.)
   - The UAE and Qatar entries stay provisional: whether their law is silent on rounding is UNVERIFIED and Q-601 covers Saudi Arabia only. Each entry carries a `provisional` note saying so.
   - **A provisional entry is inactive in production**: it authorises no `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` until it carries a `resolution` with exactly `status` (`"resolved"`), `by` (founder login), `at` (date, same rules as a blocker resolution) and `ref` (a nonblank reference to the evidence that settled it). Entries stay on the list, marked provisional, so the shipped UAE and Qatar rounding defaults are refused in production until then. `resolution` on a non-provisional entry makes the file unusable. Without a usable founders map no provisional entry is active.
2. **Q2, option A: open blockers refuse production.** A pack is refused while any `goLiveBlockers` or `pendingRefs` entry is open, or while any `UNVERIFIED` `factsOutsideSchema` entry names a locked path. An entry is open until it carries a resolution record; only a valid record stops it blocking.
   - Record format, a `resolution` object inside the entry in `evidence.json`, with exactly these keys: `status` (must be `"resolved"`), `by` (a founder GitHub login from the `founders` map in `scripts/lanes.json`, matched case-insensitively) and `at` (a real `YYYY-MM-DD` date, not in the future, not before 2026-01-01). Any other key, a missing key, or a wrong value leaves the entry refused. The existing `status` field on a blocker is an evidence label and is unchanged.
   - `factsOutsideSchema` entries name pack paths in an optional `paths` list (full pack-root dotted paths, which must exist). Scope is explicit: an `UNVERIFIED` entry whose `paths` is missing, not a list or empty is refused, because silence must not read as "concerns nothing"; it loads only if it lists paths and none is locked. Entries with a sourced label are unaffected. Sourced labels on these entries follow the same source rule as `facts`.
   - Resolving a blocker records a decision; it does not check that the underlying fix was made. The founder who signs `resolution.by` verifies that.
3. **Lows from the #282 review (all production-only record checks).**
   - Evidence status: `evidenceStatus: verified_official` needs every locked leaf backed by a `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL` fact or a listed product-choice default. A leaf backed only by `SECONDARY` facts refuses. `secondary` keeps accepting any non-`UNVERIFIED` label.
   - JSON hygiene: `pack.json`, `manifest.json`, `evidence.json` and the manifest contract parse with duplicate keys refused (this applies to `pack.json` in every environment, as it is read before any environment rule). Nesting too deep for the parser, or for a validator, is a clean refusal, not a crash.
   - Manifest meaning: `kind` must be `rigid`, `certification.touchesLedgerOrTax` must be `true`, `lifecycle.status` must not be `retired`, `certification.certifiedAt` must not be in the future, and every `requires` entry must resolve to a registered provider: a manifest under `components/` (excluding country packs) that passes the component contract in full, matches the component id or provides the capability, and has a release version inside `versionRange`. A prerelease provider never satisfies a range. The range comparator is strict and small: `||` between alternatives, single spaces between comparators, each of `>=`, `<=`, `>`, `<`, `=` (or none), `^`, `~` followed by a full `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; wildcards, partial versions, prereleases, hyphen ranges and other spacing are refused as unparsable. Malformed `requires` entries or provider manifests are refusals, never exceptions.
   - Closed key set: `evidence.json` permits only `packId`, `version`, `purpose`, `facts`, `factsOutsideSchema`, `pendingRefs`, `goLiveBlockers`, `arabicLabels`, `arabicReviewNote` and `certification`. Any other key (for example `revoked`) refuses.
   - People: `owner.github` and every `reviewedBy` entry must be a founder login (the `founders` map in `scripts/lanes.json`, matched case-insensitively; ADR-0034 requires a human reviewer and this repo has two). `certification.founder` must be the founder that `owner.github` maps to; the other founder reviews, and the existing rule that the owner cannot review their own pack stays. An unreadable or invalid founders map refuses.
   - Hygiene: a read source's `accessed` date may not be before 2026-01-01 (the project's work starts in 2026-11); the date is a named constant.

## Alternatives rejected
- **Allow `PLATFORM-DEFAULT` on any locked path with a `ref` (ADR-0063's interim rule).** A `ref` proves nothing about the choice being ours rather than the law's.
- **Put the list inside each pack.** The pack under review would name its own exceptions. The list lives beside the loader, outside every pack version.
- **Treat a pack's own blocker `status` as resolution.** That field already holds an evidence label; a separate `resolution` record keeps who and when auditable.
- **Resolver is any named person.** A founder login ties the resolution to the same identity binding as the approval; an accountant is already recorded in `certification.accountant`.

## Consequences
- The four shipped packs stay refused in production (open `pendingRefs` and `goLiveBlockers`, unlisted or provisional defaults) and load in sandbox and test. No pack JSON changes in this row.
- The founder approving a pack is its manifest owner; the other founder is its reviewer. A pack owned and reviewed by one person cannot pass.
- Per-market list entries are reviewed one at a time. Adding one, resolving a provisional one, or settling Q-601 is a data change plus a test update.
- Still true, as in ADR-0063: these checks validate recorded approvals, not accountant credentials or signatures. Tier 3 line-by-line review and a different-model security review are required before merge; neither is claimed here.
