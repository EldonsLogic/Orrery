## Hub71+ AI
- Package (research/05, official page 2026-11-04): AED 250K in-kind, AED 250K cash via a SAFE, top-up up to AED 250K for high performers for more equity. Cap, discount and equity % not published; ask Hub71. https://www.hub71.com/program/hub71-plus-ai (403 on 2026-11-21).
- Perks: free housing, office space, health insurance, 40+ capital partners, ADGM regulatory support. Cloud credits not confirmed. https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=45930 (updated 2026-07-24, secondary).
- Requirements: founders building from Abu Dhabi, in-person or hybrid by track; AI must be core to the product to unlock Hub71+ AI. ADGM entity per research/05; not re-read on an official page.
- IP: no terms found. UNVERIFIED; read the SAFE and programme agreement.
- Deadline conflict, best reading: Cohort 20 deadline was 2 Aug 2026, start Feb 2027, review Jun-Nov 2026 (search result plus secondary page). research/05 reported "deadline February 2027, start September 2027" from the official page on 2026-11-04. These fit together if the Feb 2027 date is the deadline for the cohort after Cohort 20, and applications are rolling (late applications roll to the next cohort). UNVERIFIED because the official page could not be re-read. Founders should open the portal https://go.hub71.com/Cohort20 and confirm.
- Fit: only the UAE of our four markets, but Abu Dhabi is the strongest AI signal and pairs with Shorooq (shortlist #4). Relocation of both founders is the cost.
- Call: WAIT until the spike is done and a gate opens fundraising. Applying early costs little because applications roll; relocation and the SAFE terms are the real decisions.

## Antler MENAP
- Official page: $180K SAFE for 11% equity, follow-on up to $320K uncapped, homepage says "up to $500K". Residencies in Dubai (spring) and Riyadh (fall); full-time, in person, no outside commitments; founders arrange their own visas; no fee. Teams of two or three preferred. A company that already raised equity may be affected. https://www.antler.co/residency/menap
- Next intake: official start-dates page lists no Riyadh or Dubai 2027 cohort (search summary); last listed Riyadh start 13 Apr 2026. https://www.antler.co/cohort-start-dates
- IP terms: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- Fit: we have a team and a defined product; 11% for $180K is expensive against the Hub71 SAFE and weakens the pre-seed round. Full-time residency conflicts with a spike and pilot work.
- Call: SKIP unless a founder is dropped out of day work and wants Gulf network only. Re-check if Antler publishes 2027 dates.

Equity-free is reported by a secondary guide, not QSTP. https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/251814/nation/qstp-launches-revamped-incubation-programme-with-fall-2026-cohort , https://qstp.org.qa/spring-nest
- Entry needs research, technology or innovation focus and a Qatar presence. No cash, intake date or IP terms confirmed.
- Call: SKIP now; revisit when Qatar becomes the next market. QDB route is on the shortlist (#18).

## What founders must decide
1. Relocation: is either founder willing to be based in Abu Dhabi (Hub71) or resident in KSA (Flat6Labs, Sanabil)? Without that, no programme here is open to us.
2. Dilution ceiling for a pre-seed programme: Antler at 11% is the benchmark; Hub71 and Sanabil percentages must be asked for before anything else.
3. Entity structure: a holdco that works for both ADGM (Hub71) and Saudi investors (see research/05).
4. Timing: apply only after the spike gives a demo and the gate allows fundraising; Hub71 rolls, so waiting costs one cohort at most.
5. Who emails Hub71 for the SAFE terms and the real deadline (a human step; no personal contacts recorded here).
