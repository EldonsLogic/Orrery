Evidence on how owner-led firms buy is thin (UNVERIFIED, [../research/02-market-and-customer.md](../research/02-market-and-customer.md) section 2). The first job is to test it. Free-first at launch; pricing hypotheses in [../strategy/07-pricing.md](../strategy/07-pricing.md).

## Channels (hypotheses)
| Channel | Why | First move | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accountants and VAT consultants | Likely shortlist influencers; ZATCA wave notices drive them (UNVERIFIED) | Accountant role/view in product; partner programme outline; ask them in interviews | Accountant veto if we threaten their work |
| Implementers as partners | Incumbent route (Odoo, SAP B1, NetSuite sell via partners) | Offer a "platform partner" model: they do discovery and training, not custom code | Pulls us toward software-house behaviour |
| Referral (owner networks, trade associations) | Trust and wasta in family/trade networks (UNVERIFIED) | Pilot customers as references; case studies with permission | Slow |
| Compliance-triggered inbound | Wave and e-invoicing notices create urgency | Compliance pages, guides, readiness checker ([../product/website.md](../product/website.md)) | Price-shoppers for cheap invoicing apps |
| Programmes and competitions | Visibility and validation | Dubai championship entry (founder decided; narrow agent) | Distraction |
Rejected for now: paid ads at scale, cold outbound volume, marketplaces.

## 25-interview plan
| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Goal | Validate pain, buyer, price range, accountant veto, channel; apply kill criteria (fewer than 8 describe a current ERP/compliance pain they would pay to fix; fewer than 5 would pay at SAR 2K+/month; accountant veto in more than half) from [../strategy/02-niche-and-launch-country.md](../strategy/02-niche-and-launch-country.md) |
| Mix | About 15 owners/GMs in trading and distribution, 7 accountants or VAT consultants, 3 implementers/partners; split KSA and UAE |
| Sourcing | Founders' networks, accountant referrals, LinkedIn, trade associations (names TBD) |
| Script areas | Current tools and spend; last software purchase and who decided; ZATCA/e-invoicing status; Odoo or other pain; reaction to the chat-built ERP idea and to the month simulation; price ladder (Van Westendorp-style) at SAR 2K / 3.5K / 5K; setup fee tolerance; trust in AI for books; data/hosting worries; Arabic preference |
| Rules | No pitching; record verbatim quotes without personal data; anonymise notes; store in `docs/research/` with consent |
| Output | Interview log table, findings per question, decision (continue/change niche) recorded in `docs/decisions/` |

## Onboarding and customer success outline
| Stage | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-sale | Demo with template Blueprint; quote | Founder |
| Kick-off | Discovery chat, source connection, gap questions | Platform + founder |
| Build | Human review gate, sandbox, simulation | Team |
| Go-live | Checklist, training session in Arabic/English, hypercare window | Founder |
| First 30 days | Weekly check-in; track chat usage, approvals, errors | Founder |
| Ongoing | Monthly usage review, regulation notices, change requests via chat | Founder |
| Metrics | Time to live; autonomy rate; tickets per tenant; weekly active users; NPS (later); reuse of components ([../strategy/08-product-vs-software-house.md](../strategy/08-product-vs-software-house.md)) | |
Support process (hours, channels, SLAs) is TBD for G3.
