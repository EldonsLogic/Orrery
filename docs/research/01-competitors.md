# 01 - Competitor landscape: AI-native ERP builder for owner-led SMEs in KSA/UAE

Research date: 2026-11-04. Every URL below was accessed 2026-11-04 (search result or fetched page). Convention: **[F]** = fetched/read from the vendor or primary page; **[S]** = from a search snippet or third-party page; **UNVERIFIED** = single weak source, conflicting sources, or inferred. No pricing, funding or features are invented; where I found nothing, it says "not found".

Honest method caveat: search tooling returned summaries, not full pages, for many items. Treat every `[S]` number as "re-check before putting in a deck".

---

## 0. Bottom line (read this first)

1. **The "AI-native ERP" money is real but aimed at US mid-market finance teams, not MENA SMEs.** Rillet, Campfire, DualEntry have >$100M each, none shows ZATCA/UAE e-invoicing, Arabic, or Hijri/RTL. They are not your near-term competitor; they are your future competitor if they go global.
2. **The real competitors are cheap, ZATCA-compliant, Arabic-native local SaaS** (Qoyod, Wafeq, Daftra, Rewaa, Zoho Books, Foodics for F&B) priced around SAR 99-300/month, and **Odoo + local partners** (cheap licence, SAR/USD-thousands of implementation). The owner's alternative is not "SAP"; it is "my accountant uses Qoyod/Wafeq/Odoo" or "Excel + WhatsApp".
3. **Vibe-coding builders (Lovable, Bolt, Replit) are the biggest conceptual threat**: an owner can already prompt an app. They have zero accounting integrity, ZATCA, audit trail or Arabic-business defaults, but they own the "AI builds my software" narrative and distribution.
4. **Compliance is the moat and the cost centre.** ZATCA Phase 2 now covers basically every VAT-registered Saudi business (Wave 24, deadline 30 Jun 2026); UAE e-invoicing pilot started 1 Jul 2026 with go-live 1 Jan 2027 (large) / 1 Jul 2027 (smaller). Becoming a ZATCA-integrated solution and a UAE Accredited Service Provider (or partnering with one) is mandatory table stakes, not a feature.
5. **Incumbents' AI is real but gated by price, partners and licensing** (SAP Joule, NetSuite Next, Dynamics Copilot, Agentforce); SME Gulf owners rarely touch them without a partner.

## E. Where we are most exposed (blunt)

1. **Compliance liability.** ZATCA Phase 2 (signing, QR, Fatoora integration, wave rules) and UAE PINT AE/ASP flow are non-negotiable, ongoing engineering and certification work. Qoyod, Wafeq, Zoho, Odoo, Daftra already have it; you are starting at zero. If an AI-assembled invoice component is wrong, the owner gets fined, not you - and trust is gone.
2. **"Owners won't leave their accountant's tool."** In owner-led SMEs the external accountant often decides (Qoyod/Wafeq/Zoho are accountant-friendly). Your pitch to the owner must survive the accountant's veto. UNVERIFIED but widely true in this segment; validate with 20 interviews.
3. **Price anchor is SAR ~100-200/month** for a ZATCA-compliant accounting tool (Wafeq pricing fetched; Daftra and Zoho unverified). A builder with LLM costs per chat turn must hit that with margin. Odoo at ~$9-21/user is the other anchor.
4. **Well-funded locals will add AI faster than you can build a moat.** Rewaa ($45M, AI roadmap), Foodics ($170M+, Norma AI, AWS), Wafeq ($7.5M). Expect "AI assistant" features in all of them within 12 months.
5. **Vibe-coding platforms own the narrative and have billions** (Lovable $13.3B). If they add accounting templates + ZATCA plugins via partners, they eat the low end.
6. **Arabic quality is hard and unproven for you.** Dialect (Gulf vs Egyptian vs Levantine), mixed Arabic/English, Hijri dates, RTL tables and bilingual invoices; LLM accuracy on accounting terms in Arabic is untested by us. No evidence found in this research that any competitor has solved Arabic-chat ERP configuration - good for us, but it may mean it is harder than it looks.
7. **Trust, data residency, PDPL.** Saudi PDPL and hosting requirements for financial data not researched here; a gap in this document. Incumbents (Microsoft, SAP, Oracle) have local datacenters; a startup must answer this in sales calls.
8. **"AI builds the ERP" = unbounded test surface.** Every owner's configuration differs; bugs in generated workflows look like accounting errors. Odoo's partners and Zoho's templates are deterministic and auditable by comparison.
9. **Distribution.** Odoo/Zoho/Dynamics partners, Zid/Salla app stores and accountants are the real channels; a new brand has none.

## F. Gaps to exploit

1. **Time-to-ERP**: Odoo/partner implementations take weeks and thousands; Qoyod/Wafeq are pure accounting. Promise "operational in a day from a WhatsApp-style Arabic chat" - but prove it on a narrow set of 3-4 business types (trading/wholesale, services, contracting, clinics) first. Daftra offers ~50 industry versions already, so pick verticals Daftra serves badly (to be validated).
2. **Arabic-first conversational configuration** (not UI translation): no competitor found offers it for ERP setup (UNVERIFIED; absence of evidence in limited search).
3. **Beyond accounting**: Qoyod/Wafeq stop at accounting; Odoo needs a partner; Dynamics/NetSuite are too big. Gap = owner-operable approvals, inventory, projects, HR/WPS payroll in one system, assembled from reusable components.
4. **Pre-certified component library** as the differentiator versus Lovable/Bolt: AI may only compose vetted components (ledger, VAT, ZATCA invoice, Mudad/WPS, Hijri calendar). This turns the biggest weakness of vibe-coding (no compliance) into your moat and answers risk #8.
5. **Compliance as a service for UAE**: with go-live dates 2027 and ASP deadlines still confusing owners, an "we handle e-invoicing for you via an accredited ASP" bundle can be a wedge for smaller UAE companies (July 2027 go-live per sources, UNVERIFIED dates).
6. **Partner-friendly angle**: sell to the 100s of Odoo/Zoho implementers and accountants as a delivery accelerator (they configure with AI, keep the customer relationship) rather than fighting them. Needs a partner map (not yet done).
7. **Pricing wedge**: usage-light, flat SAR tier near Wafeq Premium (SAR 199/mo) including ZATCA; avoid per-token pricing that scares owners (contrast Replit/Bolt credits and Agentforce metering, which owners hate).
8. **Migration**: DualEntry claims 24-hour migration in the US; nobody found does AI-led migration from Qoyod/Excel/old Arabic desktop software (Al-Ameen, etc. - not researched) in the Gulf.
