## 3. Cross-cutting design implications (derived, not legal claims)

- Wave/phase rules are moving targets (UAE deadline changed twice in 2026; Wave thresholds step down). Keep dates, thresholds and rates in effective-dated config tables, not code.
- KSA integration is direct to ZATCA; UAE integration goes via an ASP. These are different build tracks.
- Store Arabic and English legal names separately; tax invoices must be reliably RTL/bilingual in PDF and XML.
- Archive signed XML + rendered PDF immutably; retention configurable per country.
- Hosting: offer KSA-region and UAE-region tenants; document sub-processors and transfer mechanism.

## 5. Open legal questions for a lawyer

1. Does ZATCA require any formal vendor registration, approval or contractual relationship for e-invoicing solution providers in 2026 beyond SDK/sandbox conformity?
2. Confirm UAE ASP accreditation rules and whether an ERP vendor may integrate with an ASP without being one; liability split.
3. Confirm the final UAE schedule (MD 66 of 2026; guidelines v1.1 conflict) and B2C exclusion.
4. Arabic language and Hijri date requirements for invoices in both countries; which fields must be Arabic.
5. Retention periods (KSA VAT/zakat/ZATCA e-invoice, UAE VAT/CT) and who bears archiving duty.
6. PDPL: which transfer mechanism is acceptable now for hosting outside KSA; is a KSA-region host mandatory for ERP data; need to register with SDAIA as processor; DPO.
7. Do NCA ECC/CCC or CST cloud rules apply to us as a SaaS vendor serving private SMEs, and to which clients?
8. UAE PDPL Executive Regulations status and transfer rules; DIFC/ADGM scope.
9. Small Business Relief extension (Decision 131 of 2026 claim) and other UAE CT changes; GOSI step-up schedule; Emiratisation contribution amounts.
10. AI features: use of client data for model training/third-party LLM processing across borders under PDPL.
11. Whether payroll file generation or tax data preparation could make us a regulated service (e.g. tax agent, WPS channel).
12. Need for local entity/Commercial Registration to contract with Saudi/UAE customers and for invoicing in SAR/AED.
