## 2. Accelerators and programmes

### Hub71 / Hub71+ AI (Abu Dhabi) - VERIFIED-OFFICIAL
- Stage pre-seed to Series A; AI must be core to the product; at least one founder must relocate long-term and build team in Abu Dhabi.
- Package: AED 250,000 in-kind incentives, AED 250,000 cash provided for equity via a SAFE, and a top-up of up to AED 250,000 for high performers at end of year for additional equity. Equity %, cap and discount not published (ask Hub71).
- Timing as shown on the Hub71+ AI page when fetched: deadline February 2027, review by April 2027, feedback within 3 months, programme starts September 2027, runs 12 months. Late applicants roll to the next cohort. Search-result snippet of the same site had shown a different cohort (deadline 2 Aug 2026, start Feb 2027); the fetched page is the later state, but re-check.
- Selection: application, team meeting, partner meeting, committee.
- Source: https://www.hub71.com/program/hub71-plus-ai (2026-11-04). Equity detail commentary: https://theincubatorpro.com/programs/hub71/ (third party).
- Fit: high for AI tag and UAE cash. Cost: relocation and SAFE dilution, and Abu Dhabi entity (ADGM).

## 3. Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy: Create AI Agents Championship

- Launched 7 October 2026 by Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed (per press); successor to the Create Apps Championship. REPORTED.
- Categories: Best Societal, Best Personal, Best Business, Best Youth AI Agent. **Best Business AI Agent is where we compete.**
- Grants: each category winner receives AED 550,000 (listed as USD 150,000 on the site); AI Agent of the Year rises to AED 1 million per Dubai Media Office; total pot cited as above AED 2.5M or USD 750,000 depending on source (sources conflict). Entry free; entrants fund own development.
- Eligibility: open worldwide, teams up to six, each member registers individually; idea, prototype or early MVP accepted; existing companies may enter only to pitch a new agent or capability that is not already fully on the market; English materials; agent must be original and protect data.
- Timeline: registration opened 7 Oct 2026; closes December 2026 (one blog says Dec/Jan); prototype, pitch deck and demo video due January 2027; semi-finalists Feb-Mar 2027; 12 finalists; grand final first week of May 2027 with a live working agent. Explore phase Oct-Nov, build phase Nov 2026-Jan 2027.
- IP ownership and equity: NOT STATED in the sources I read. Read the official rules before entering. The official page (dubaichamberdigital.com/en/create-ai-agents) was unreachable from my tooling (connection refused), so terms come from press and a tbreak summary.
- Sources: https://tbreak.com/create-ai-agents-championship-dubai/ , https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/dubai-launches-global-create-ai-170500743.html (as listed in search results), https://www.dubaichamberdigital.com/en/create-ai-agents (2026-11-04).
- Fit note: an "AI agent" entry for an ERP product should be a new agent capability (for example an owner-led SME finance or procurement agent), not the whole product. Registration closes within about a month, so decide this week. Non-dilutive, large relative to a pre-seed cheque, and gives Dubai government visibility.

## 4. What investors expect at our stage

Mostly my synthesis plus the weak sources noted; no primary investor statements were verified in this pass, so treat as hypotheses to test in meetings.

- **Traction.** Pre-seed: design partners or LOIs and a working demo; paying pilots help. Seed: repeat revenue from owner-led SMEs, with logo concentration shown honestly. Comparable Gulf pre-seeds in the table above carried $0.5M-$3.7M rounds on early traction (UNVERIFIED detail). The gap in comps means a US AI-native ERP narrative (Rillet) will be used to anchor, so show why Gulf SMEs (Arabic, VAT/ZATCA e-invoicing, WPS, local banks) need a local product. Regulatory specifics are my assumption, not sourced here.
- **Revenue mix and margins.** Expect questions on software vs services share. An "ERP builder" invites implementation-services drift; show software gross margin targets (AI inference cost per tenant, hosting) and keep services below a stated cap. Target numbers are not sourced; do not quote benchmarks to investors without a source.
- **Team.** Two founders, one technical and one with SME or ERP domain access; Gulf-based or willing to relocate (Hub71 requires it).
- **Local data residency.** Saudi investors respond to in-Kingdom data (Gaia raised on that pitch, UNVERIFIED aggregator). Design for KSA/UAE hosting.
- **Entity structure.**
  - KSA: new Investment Law (M/19) effective February 2025; sources conflict on whether the MISA "licence" was replaced by "registration"; 100% foreign ownership of an LLC is generally allowed outside restricted activities. Verify with MISA. https://rw.sa/en/knowledge/saudi-company-formation-surge-2026 , https://expectat.com/guides/company-formation-in-saudi-arabia/ , https://soveraglobal.com/company-formation-saudi-arabia/
  - UAE: ADGM SPV is described by consultancies as the preferred startup/VC holdco; one source says nexus tests were dropped for DIFC prescribed companies (24 Jul 2026) and ADGM SPVs (Sep 2026) - UNVERIFIED, check regulator. https://boruconsulting.com/blog/adgm-vs-difc-holding-company-structures , https://globallawexperts.com/difc-vs-adgm-uae/
  - **Delaware flip vs ADGM/Cayman holdco and Saudi investor preferences: NOT FOUND in my searches.** Common practice is that Saudi VCs invest in a Delaware, ADGM or Cayman holdco with a Saudi opco underneath, but I have no source. Ask a lawyer and ask Raed/Sanabil which they prefer. Note Hub71 is an Abu Dhabi/ADGM route and Sanabil/Raed are Saudi, so a dual-hub plan will need a holdco that works for both.
