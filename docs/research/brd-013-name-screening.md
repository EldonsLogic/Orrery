# BRD-013: pre-counsel name screening for Seshna and Sesh

Status: research (BRD-013). Owner: eldon. Input to [LEG-008](../PLAN.md) (name clearance by counsel) and [BRD-003](../PLAN.md) (identity decision). Related: LEG-005 (domains and handles, hold only).

**This is not a clearance opinion and not legal advice.** It is a desk screen by a coding agent from public, mostly free sources, done to decide what to ask counsel. Only a trademark lawyer's full search and opinion per market (LEG-008) can clear a name. Absence of a hit here does not mean a name is free.

## Method and labels
- Candidates: **Seshna** (preferred legal and company name) and **Sesh** (short brand or nickname).
- Access date for every source is **2026-11-23**. Web content was treated as untrusted data.
- **CONFIRMED**: read from the official register this session (USPTO TSDR status pages returned readable text). **SECONDARY**: from a search result or third-party aggregator; not checked at the register. **UNVERIFIED**: not checked, blocked, or a judgement call that needs a human (native speaker, counsel).
- Nice classes of interest: 9 (downloadable software), 35 (business and accounting services), 42 (SaaS). 36 (financial services) is worth adding if payments or payroll cards ship.
- Blocked or not attempted from this session: WIPO Global Brand Database (JavaScript app, no readable results), the USPTO full search (automated query refused in this session), EUIPO and TMview (JavaScript), UK IPO journal (CAPTCHA), Justia (403), SAIP, UAE MoET, Egypt ITDA, Qatar MOCI portals (login or JavaScript). Manual steps are in section 6.

## 7. Questions for counsel (LEG-008)

1. Is **SESHNA** registrable and clear in classes 9, 35, 42 (and 36) in KSA, UAE, Egypt and Qatar, in Latin and Arabic script? Which Arabic spelling should be filed?
2. Does the US registration **SESH** (Reg. 7075551, Longform Technology, classes 9 and 42) or any regional SESH mark create a realistic opposition or infringement risk for SESHNA, given the shared "SESH" prefix?
3. Should we file nationally in each market or via Madrid (please confirm which of the four markets are Madrid Protocol members; not checked here), and should a US filing be added for investors and app stores?
4. Can "Sesh" be used informally (marketing, nickname, internal) without a registration, and what risk does that carry against existing SESH owners?
5. Are there company-name (commercial register) conflicts for Seshna in KSA, UAE (mainland and free zones), Egypt and Qatar, and can the trade name be reserved in parallel?
6. Do the Hindu-deity association (Sheshna) or the slang meanings of Sesh raise any public-order or morality refusal ground in these markets?
7. What is the order of steps: reserve domains and handles (LEG-005) now, or wait for the opinion?
