## Identity core (PLT-005)
Package [`identity/`](identity/) holds the domain logic of [ADR-0007](../../docs/decisions/ADR-0007-identity.md), Python 3.12, standard library only. No network server, no identity provider and no real users: Q-14 blocks storing real users, and the provider and production signing come from the identity-provider spike.
- `models.py`: Person, Membership (status, session version, MFA flag), StaffIdentity in a separate realm, invites, refresh records, support grants.
- `tokens.py` + `signing.py`: short-lived access tokens (person, one tenant, audience naming that tenant, issuer, realm, session version, MFA, auth time, expiry; no roles). Algorithm pinned to the realm's signer, `none` refused, header and claim set exact, constant-time signature check, injected clock. `TestOnlyHmacSha256Signer` is for tests only.
- `service.py`: token issue and verify per realm, per-membership session version (removal, suspension, compromise and tenant suspension revoke at once), refresh rotation with reuse detection that revokes the family, MFA at issue and on role grant, owner-only "MFA for all".
- `policy.py`: roles and permissions come only from an injected `RoleResolver` (the tenant's assignment store, `roles_for` and `permissions_for`); any unexpected result denies. MFA mandatory for `isAdmin`, finance, accounting, owner and all staff.
- `invites.py`: single-use, expiring, bound to a channel the accepting person has verified; needs the `member.invite` permission, resolved for the verified inviter from the `RoleResolver`. A re-admitted member continues from the per-person and tenant session high-water mark, so old tokens stay dead; removal also tombstones every refresh family.
- `support.py`: staff just-in-time grants, time-boxed, approved by the tenant's owner or admin with a fresh MFA step-up, never self-approved, tenant-visible audit.
- Refresh family revocation is a durable tombstone checked on every rotation and refused atomically by `put_refresh`. Owner "MFA for all" changes and grant approval need an MFA step-up within 300 s; staff calls re-check the staff identity, session version and tenant status.
- `audit.py`: append-only in-memory decisions (allow/deny, IDN code); `repository.py`: abstract store plus an in-memory test store.
- `identity/mfa/` (SEC-005): TOTP (RFC 6238, stdlib only, one step of drift, replay refused by a last-used counter advanced with compare-and-set; algorithm, digits and period stored per factor), single-use recovery codes (runtime-generated, per-code-salted PBKDF2 hashes), `MfaService.sign_in` (password then factor, token with mfa=true and auth_time=now), `step_up`, enrolment and removal. Rules (PR #186 review): every sign-in failure is IDN-060 and the factor work (TOTP slots, a fixed number of PBKDF2 recovery slots) runs whether or not the password matched; membership state shows only after password and factor match, and before the factor is consumed. A customer's first factor needs the password proven now: a session whose `auth_time` is within 300 s (refresh keeps the old `auth_time`) or the password re-entered in `begin_totp_enrolment`; further factors, removal, recovery regeneration and staff factors need an MFA step-up. Staff bootstrap uses a single-use admin ticket that expires (`bootstrap_ttl`), counts attempts and answers IDN-060 for every failure. Any change of active factors ends every session of the person in every tenant (or the staff member's) and re-derives `mfa_enrolled`; the last factor is removed only through the atomic `FactorRepository.remove_factor(keep_one)`, refused when any of the person's tenants demands MFA. Password guesses count per (subject, source) and an optional `SourceThrottle`; the account lock (IDN-061) counts only password-proven attempts. Store, sealing and provider errors are IDN-064 and not counted. Contracts in `ports.py`: `SecretBox` must be AEAD bound to realm, subject and factor id. **Blockers:** no sign-in or bootstrap route may be exposed until the gateway supplies `source` and a `SourceThrottle` (lockout and enumeration residuals, Q-224, Q-251), and an ADR for the MFA design is required before SEC-005 is accepted (Q-250). Open: Q-220 to Q-224, Q-250, Q-251.

Errors are `IdentityError` with codes IDN-001 to IDN-064 ([`errors.py`](identity/errors.py)); messages never carry secrets. Open points: Q-147 to Q-151 in [open-questions](../../docs/open-questions.md). Hardening (Q-152 to Q-154 closed, Q-155 partly): `AccessClaims` (alias `VerifiedClaims`) is registered only by `tokens.decode`; direct construction, `replace`, copies and pickling raise IDN-006, and every service call re-checks (`check_live`) that the exact object, of the exact type and with unchanged values, came from `decode` and is inside its nbf/exp window, so a kept token stops working at expiry. This is a best-effort in-process guard; the signature is the boundary. Support grants move through `Repository.transition_grant` (compare-and-set that sets only the named fields; a real store must make it one atomic conditional write), so a revoke always beats a racing approve, keeps the approval fields, and a second approval cannot extend expiry. Pending requests die after 24 h (checked on approve and on use) and are capped at 5 per staff member and tenant through the atomic `Repository.put_grant_if_under_cap` (IDN-045). `support.revoke` needs the same MFA step-up as approve; audit `detail` is capped at 200 characters and verify decisions record the subject. Still open from Q-155 (must close before a real store or any route): single-use or action-bound step-up, `for_tenant` keyed on caller input, gateway collapse of IDN-031/032/033 and IDN-020/021, role-gate dependence on `check_role_grant` and stale `mfa_required`, key rotation (single `kid`), 7-day `auth_time` at issue, and caller authorisation on revocation (Q-149).

## Human review queue (PLT-013, Tier 3)
Package [`reviewqueue/`](reviewqueue/) (standard library only, builds on `identity`): the queue behind the `team_reviewed` gate ([ADR-0049](../../docs/decisions/ADR-0049-human-review-queue.md), Proposed). Items (Blueprint draft, component request, simulation sign-off evidence) are append-only logs of HMAC-sealed, chained events; state is folded on every read, never stored. Verified staff humans with the role `team_reviewer` claim (expiring lock) and decide (approved, changes_requested, rejected) after a fresh MFA step-up; the author, requester, attesters, signers and linked staff cannot review their own item. Decisions bind the content digest (stale on change), checklists are data, held items cannot be approved, audit lines hold note hash and length only. `gate_evidence` and `team_reviewed_evidence` are evidence for BPL-020 and never promote a build. No key, no queue. Open: Q-440 to Q-446.

## Feature flags (PLT-016)
Package [`featureflags/`](featureflags/), stdlib only. Boolean flags that decide whether a code path is reached; no value, so nothing in a flag can be a secret or a rate. The checks behind the path (permissions, human floor, deterministic tax, ledger and payroll code) still run: a flag never grants a permission or role and never alters tax, ledger, payroll or e-invoice maths.
- Fail closed: an unknown or malformed key, an unverified or expired context, a bad or backwards clock, a store error or any exception gives OFF, even for a flag whose default is ON. `FlagEvaluator.evaluate` never raises. A far-future clock reading fails only that call (believed only if the next reading agrees); an optional `revoked` port turns a revoked session into OFF.
- Order: platform kill, flag kill, tenant kill (uncached, always win), then global ON, user rule, tenant rule, deterministic percentage (sha256; basis points; growing it only adds units), explicit global OFF, then the definition default. Global OFF does not override a tenant, user or percent rule; clear a rule to remove it, `set_global(None)` returns to the default.
- Context: `EvalContext` holds only verified `AccessClaims`; tenant and user are read from the claims on every call, never from stored fields, and the object is immutable. Rules are per tenant, so no tenant sees or inherits another's. Cache is keyed by the store version (a write shows on the next evaluation), bounded (1024 entries, TTL at most 60 s).
- Tier 3 change authority (restricted flags: `agent_authority`, `money`, `compliance`; they cannot default ON). Enabling one (global, tenant, user, percent increase) and releasing the platform-wide kill need a proposer and a different approver: each staff-realm, MFA, holding the configured staff role (`restricted_role` and `staff_roles` port; none configured means refuse), each spending a fresh step-up that is consumed (one step-up, one change). The approval takes only a proposal id, so it is bound to the exact change (flag, value, scope, 15-minute expiry), is single use and a proposer cannot approve or burn their own. Switching OFF, engaging a kill, standard flags and flag or tenant kill release (one person, consumed step-up) stay single-person.
- A proposal is also bound to the state it was made against (the flag's rules and the kill epoch): an incident that turns the flag off or changes a kill voids it, so a release proposed in incident 1 cannot release incident 2. Releasing a flag kill on a restricted flag needs the role. Each proposer has a quota and platform-release proposals have reserved slots. A flag's restricted category is pinned in sealed config and cannot be downgraded by a code change. Writes refuse when a key named in the log is missing.
- "Never an agent" is not proven by claims (no human flag, Q-450, Q-431). It rests on realm separation, the staff role given only to named humans, a refusal of subject ids containing agent-like tokens (normalised; plus an optional allow-pattern from identity) and the second human.
- Audit: every change is an append-only record (who, approver, when, old, new, reason) sealed with a keyed HMAC (key id stored, key required) and anchored (length and head kept apart from the log); every read verifies and fails closed on truncation or rewrite. Tenants get a redacted view of their own changes only (no staff ids or free text, never platform-wide records). Reasons refuse control, format (bidi, zero-width) and separator characters. One clock (the identity service's); record time never goes backwards.
- Admin labels are i18n keys (`flags.<key>.label`, `.description`); `labels.missing` fails when English or Arabic is absent.
- Review tier: Tier 3 (it gates agent-authority, money and compliance paths). Open: Q-450 to Q-455.
